About QEVRI

Discover. Compare. Choose.

QEVRI is designed to make online product discovery feel simple, useful and enjoyable.

Shopping should feel like discovery.

Instead of visiting multiple websites and searching through thousands of listings, QEVRI brings interesting products together in one discovery-focused experience.

You can explore categories, search for products, use budget filters, discover popular products and then go directly to the original retailer when you find something you like.

Discover Find products across different categories.
Compare See prices, ratings and product information before deciding.
Choose Continue to the retailer when you are ready.
Personalize Use QEVRI Match and budget filters to narrow your discovery.

Why QEVRI?

QEVRI focuses on the journey before the purchase. The goal is not to overwhelm visitors with endless products, but to make discovering useful products feel easier.

QEVRI can also surface products that are receiving more attention from visitors, allowing the discovery experience to change naturally over time.
